## Superseded

Earlier deployments are recorded in the repo under `deployments/monad-testnet-10143/`,
including two that were broken. They are kept deliberately: one could accept deposits and
never pay anything out, and anyone who finds that address should know not to use it.

<Warning>
`0x8Ea29D5C3eed4Bc6D8E68c25065f6E30BDE74464` is a **one-way vault**. Its exit path was never
bound, so collateral can go in and nothing can come out. Do not use it.
</Warning>
